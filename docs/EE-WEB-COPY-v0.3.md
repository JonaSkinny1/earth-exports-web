### 2. The Manifest `/manifest`

**Job:** Identity, craft, and legal clarity.

**H1**  
The Manifest

**Lead**  
We are not a ministry. We are a house mark.

Earth Exports operates from the Ohio Outpost on Sol-3. Fifty years after formal contact, the valuable thing Earth puts on a ship is not only ore — it is culture you can hold: games, tools, documents, and objects made by hand in a gravity well.

We publish dossier decks drawn from our Archive of First Contact (AFC), finish field relics as display pieces, and sell fabrication schematics for licensed builders. Filing stamps you will see — PTC, AFC, TCRD, Sol-3 Consular Authority — are house fiction. They keep the shelves honest. They are not government credentials.

**Why Ohio**  
Ohio is not a compromise. It is the outpost. The same region that pours steel and runs a flea table runs this merchant house. Crates that don’t ship go under the tent at the local flea. Commissions get a real address and a real reply.

**House rule**  
Nothing here is issued by a government. Everything here is built to look like it survived one — as design, not as deception.

**Disclaimer panel** (bordered, visible — not footer-only)  
Earth Exports is a work of speculative fiction and independent design. We are not affiliated with any government, military, or scientific agency. Products are original decorative and maker goods. They are not authentic government, military, or intelligence documents.

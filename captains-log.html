News & Captain’s Log

Short, dated updates from Earth Exports: what’s new, what’s in the works and where to find us. Newest first.

Ask us a question

Entries marked “Real news” have real Earth dates and are true shop updates. Entries marked “Story” are in-world logs dated only by the Trade Year (TY 2076, fifty years ahead) and a ship’s day. They’re fiction.

  1. Story · TY 2076 · Day 341

    Vex tried the crew lock again

    A familiar voice asked the crew deck for the code. It was our own voice, which is how we knew. Vex is a Mimic: he copies voices, faces, goods and seals, and only his two dark eye spots never change. He typed the code, said “i got you,” and walked straight into the Manifest check. The Helion vault stays locked.

  2. Oct 12, 2026 Real news

    Smuggler files issued

    the dark species cards are now called Smuggler cards, next to the Earth file cards, on the Cards & Games page. In the story, a Smuggler file is the off-the-record meeting: a bigger reward, and a fair chance the species’ weakness or rival wrecks the deal. The Merchant Starter and the Beyond Sol expansion still have no release date. Preorder questions are welcome.

  3. Story · TY 2076 · Day 338

    Ventkin and Icecutters argue about water

    Geyser round at Saturn Rings. The Icecutters had fresh-cut ice and called the Ventkin’s brine old-fashioned. On Europa the Ventkin called the Icecutters reckless and their ice half-melted. Both say theirs is fresher. We bought from neither. The quartermaster kept a sealed Lab quarantine jar empty, just in case.

  4. Story · TY 2076 · Day 336

    Toll paid at the edge

    The Edgewardens weighed our cargo at Charon and sold us a weigh-stone, so one good now has a fixed price for everyone at the table. Twenty minutes later the Starwake Comet came through and skipped the toll. Again. The Edgewardens wrote it down, slowly.

  5. Story · TY 2076 · Day 334

    Bronzeridge fines a Mimic

    Inspection day at Kepler-452b. A Mimic arrived with perfect papers and a face that didn’t match them. Bronzeridge fined it on the spot and seized a crate of copied two-color cords. The Bisola want those back. The Mimic slumped into a puddle until someone new walked by, then sold the whole story to The Hushed as a secret.

  6. Story · TY 2076 · Day 331

    Lodestar escort hired

    Two Lodestars rode with us past Jupiter: built robots with helmet heads and a visor slit, paid in metal only. Their shield kept one canister safe the whole trip. A Junkwright skiff followed for a while, eyeing their magnets. The Lodestars waved. The skiff left.

  7. Story · TY 2076 · Day 329

    Darkreed only count in threes

    We brought four crates to Kepler-186f. The Darkreed bought three and refused the fourth: sets of 3, 6 or 9, no leftovers. An Amberhive trader at the next stall offered to take it, then raised the price because it was our second deal in a row. Loyalty pays, apparently.

  8. Story · TY 2076 · Day 326

    The Kindled answered for free

    The Kindled told us the next storm before we asked. One free answer per visit. The Hushed, who sell answers for secrets, did not speak to us for the rest of the run.

  9. Sep 25, 2026 Real news

    the Alien Index lists all twenty-three species, grouped into Sol System and Beyond Sol. Each file has a home world, a scientific name and a link to that species on the star map. The Forgotten Men have already asked for a copy.

    the new website is up, and news will land here first. The Etsy shop link isn’t ready yet.

  12. September 2026 Real news

    the first four display props are being finished and the STL files are being test-printed. Both stay placeholders until they’re ready. Nothing ships until it passes the Manifest.

The captain’s private shelf for lore fans · tap to open

Bridge · Cosmic Voyager CV-OH-01

The captain is a traveler who collects things: antiques, rare items, knowledge, crystal books from other worlds, and living plants kept alive in propagation life-support canisters along the lounge wall.

Good people want that cargo. So do not-so-good people. That’s why sales are careful: every crate is checked against the Manifest before it leaves the ship.

The loudest of the not-so-good is a collector called Vex, a Mimic from Luyten's Star who copies voices, faces, goods and seals. He wants the Helion crystals (street name: ringheart), and he is not cleared. The vault stays locked.

F.R.A.N.K., the lounge’s bartender automaton, keeps the lamps warm and the cups full, and remembers everyone who was polite to him.

Seen at a dock market on the last run: flux resonators and phase spanners changing hands. Not for sale here. The captain just likes to look.

House identity

The Manifest

We are not a ministry. We are a house mark.

Earth Exports operates from the Youngstown Outpost on Sol-3. Fifty years after formal contact, the valuable thing Earth puts on a ship is not only ore — it is culture you can hold: games, tools, documents, and objects made by hand in a gravity well.

We publish dossier decks drawn from our Archive of First Contact (AFC), finish field relics as display pieces, and sell fabrication schematics for licensed builders. Filing stamps you will see — PTC, AFC, TCRD, Sol-3 Consular Authority — are house fiction. They keep the shelves honest. They are not government credentials.

Why Youngstown

Youngstown is not a compromise. It is the outpost. The same city that pours steel and runs a flea table runs this merchant house. Crates that don’t ship go under the tent. Commissions get a real address and a real reply.

House rule

Nothing here is issued by a government. Everything here is built to look like it survived one — as design, not as deception.

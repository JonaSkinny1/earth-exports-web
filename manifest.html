About Earth Exports

We’re a small Ohio maker shop. We design sci-fi card games, display props and 3D-print files, all set in one made-up universe, and we always label the fiction.

Contact us

What we make

  • Cards & games: alien card decks and MANIFEST, our tabletop game
  • Props & relics: weathered sci-fi display pieces, first run of four
  • STL files: 3D-print files so you can print our designs at home

Why it looks “official”

The stamps, case files and serial plates are a design style, made to look like they survived a space-age bureaucracy. It’s fiction for fun. None of it is a real government document or credential.

Disclaimer

Earth Exports is a work of speculative fiction and independent design. We are not affiliated with any government, military, or scientific agency. Products are original decorative and maker goods. They are not authentic government, military, or intelligence documents.

The story for lore fans · tap to open

In the story, Earth Exports is a merchant house at the Ohio Outpost on Sol-3, trading from the lounge of the collector freighter Cosmic Voyager. Fifty years after first contact, the valuable thing Earth puts on a ship isn’t only ore. It’s culture you can hold: games, tools, documents and objects made by hand in a gravity well.

In that story, the ship does not push through space. It holds still. One core carries its weight, and three points at the edges decide which way the world slides past it. That is why it can hover in silence and change direction at once.

The captain is a traveler who keeps living plants in canisters, and rare cargo with them. Other people want that cargo, so sales stay careful.

The filing stamps you’ll see (PTC, AFC, TCRD, Sol-3 Consular Authority) are made-up house fiction, not real agencies. House rule: nothing here is issued by a government. Everything here is built to look like it survived one, as design, not deception.

The story for lore fans · tap to open

Earth Exports is an independent Sol-3 merchant house. In the fiction it trades from the passenger lounge of Cosmic Voyager, a lived-in collector freighter with warm lamps and a big window full of home. Rare cargo ships under stamp. The crate that doesn’t ship goes on the table at the Ohio Outpost flea.

The captain collects antiques, crystal books and living plants, so sales are careful. A collector named Vex keeps asking about the Helion crystals. The answer is no.

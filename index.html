The story for lore fans · tap to open

Earth Exports is an independent Sol-3 merchant house. In the fiction it trades from the passenger lounge of Cosmic Voyager, a lived-in collector freighter with warm lamps and a big window full of home. Rare cargo ships under stamp. The crate that doesn’t ship waits at the Earth home port for pickup.

The captain collects antiques, crystal books and living plants, so sales are careful. Vex is a Mimic; it keeps asking about the Helion crystals. The answer is no.

# Earth Exports crew creed (canon, approved 2026-10-14)

## Core idea
Earth Exports crew represent the company **and all of humanity**. They inspire hope and go above and beyond to serve and provide for everyone: love, compassion, kindness, understanding, empathy, gentleness, adaptability, courage, curiosity, endurance, strength and bravery. Like a rescue crew, they're the first ones in when someone needs help, and they're always there helping build the galaxy.

Public copy rule: describe this in our own words only. No franchise names, no real organizations or military units.

## The creed
**The Earth Exports Crew**
We carry more than cargo. We carry the best of Earth.
When a dock burns, we're first in. When a colony goes dark, we bring the light back.
We're kind when it's hard, brave when it counts, and curious about everyone we meet.
Wherever you are in the galaxy, if you need a hand, look for the teal and orange.
**Earth Exports. Always there.**
